# Polkaa

> **Field Payments Infrastructure for Nigerian Businesses**

Polkaa is the simple way for Nigerian businesses to pay casual staff, interstate drivers, construction artisans, and smallholder farmers directly from a central dashboard. Workers cash out physical naira at any local shop or agent using an SMS code—no smartphone, mobile app, or bank account required.

---

## Key Features

- **Company Wallet & Float**: Fund your business wallet once. Disburse payments to thousands of workers without bank network downtime or delayed transfer alerts.
- **SMS Cash-Out**: Workers receive a secure code via SMS and collect physical cash at 1.3M+ local POS and MoMo agents nationwide.
- **48-Hour Auto-Refund**: Uncollected cash codes automatically return 100% of the funds to the company wallet after 48 hours.
- **Direct USSD (*671#)**: Retailers, distributors, and buyers pay you directly from basic button phones with zero internet connection.
- **Two-Person Approval**: Enforce dual-authorization PIN controls on batch payouts to eliminate fraud and ghost workers.
- **Interactive Sandbox & Overview Dashboard**: Live simulated payment operations, money trail analytics, and audit logs.

---

## Core Operational Sectors

1. **FMCG & Route Distribution**: Doorstep van sales collection without cash bags or highway robbery risks.
2. **Haulage & Logistics**: Roadside cash code funding for transit drivers and motor boys.
3. **Construction Sites**: Weekly bulk Excel payouts to casual site labourers with 2-signer PIN authorization.
4. **Agribusiness & Outgrowers**: Instant harvest payments sent to smallholder farmers' basic torchlight phones.
5. **Humanitarian Aid Grants**: Direct-to-beneficiary cash distributions with 100% donor reconciliation.

